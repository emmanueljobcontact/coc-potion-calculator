CLASH OF CLANS POTION MECHANICS & VALUE GUIDE

Clash of Clans potions are time multipliers. Instead of advancing an upgrade by a static number of hours instantly, they accelerate the in-game clock for 1 hour.

Builder Potion (10x Speed)

Duration: 1 Hour (Real Time)

  • How it works: In 1 real hour, builders complete 10 hours worth of work.
  • Net Time Saved: 10 hours work - 1 hour elapsed = 9 hours saved per builder.
  • Affects ALL Builders: If you have 6 builders active, 1 potion saves 54 hours of total builder time!
  • Leftover Boost: If an upgrade finishes in 26 minutes, the remaining 34 minutes of 10x boost remains active for your next upgrade!

Research Potion (24x Speed)

Duration: 1 Hour (Real Time)

  • How it works: In 1 real hour, your Laboratory completes 24 hours (1 full day) of research.
  • Net Time Saved: 24 hours work - 1 hour elapsed = 23 hours saved!
  • Sub-Hour Research: A 4h 20m lab upgrade finishes in only 10 minutes and 50 seconds!
  • Raid Medals: 3 Research Potions can be bought weekly from the Trader for 600 Raid Medals, saving 69 hours of lab time every week!

Frequently Asked Questions & Formula Details

What happens if my upgrade has 4 hours and 20 minutes left and I drink a Builder Potion?

Because the Builder Potion boosts at 10x speed, 4 hours and 20 minutes (260 minutes) is finished in exactly 260 / 10 = 26 minutes of real-world time. The remaining 34 minutes of the potion will continue to boost any other ongoing upgrades or whatever you start next!

What happens if I use a Research Potion on a 4 hours and 20 minutes lab upgrade?

With a 24x multiplier, 4 hours 20 minutes (15,600 seconds) is finished in 15,600 / 24 = 650 seconds (10 minutes and 50 seconds). You have 49 minutes and 10 seconds of 24x boost left over!

Can I stack multiple potions?

Yes! In Clash of Clans, drinking 2 Builder Potions extends the duration to 2 hours (20 hours of progress). Drinking 3 extends to 3 hours (30 hours of progress). The speed multiplier remains 10x, but the duration stacks consecutively.
